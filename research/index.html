Research

Cancer Research

Pharmaceutical and Biomedical Research

My primary research interest is focused on cancer research, particularly the investigation of potential therapeutic approaches and the identification of biologically relevant molecular targets.

I am interested in combining pharmaceutical knowledge with modern computational and data-driven approaches to understand disease mechanisms and support drug discovery.

Computational Drug Discovery

In Silico Research

I am interested in computational methods that can be used to investigate interactions between drug-like molecules and biological targets.

  • Molecular docking and virtual screening
  • Protein-ligand interaction analysis
  • Molecular dynamics simulation
  • Structure-based drug discovery
  • Computational analysis of potential therapeutic compounds
